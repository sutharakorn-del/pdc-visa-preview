Visa information

Short-stay, long-stay and special visas: which group does your case fall under?

Start with two questions — how long will you stay in Thailand, and for what purpose — and the visa picture becomes clearer.

Published 24 Sep 2026General information

01Start with two questions

Before looking at the list of visas, answer these two questions first: How long will you stay in Thailand? and What is your purpose for staying? Your answers will help rule out almost all irrelevant options

02Short-stay visa

For short trips and stays, such as tourism or visiting family. Some cases require an embassy letter as supporting documentation.

03Long-stay visa

For continuous stays with a clear reason, such as

  • Family — Thai spouse, supporting a Thai child, or dependant
  • Work
  • Retirement
  • Education

This visa group usually requires documents supporting the purpose, and has ongoing matters to handle during the stay, such as the 90-day report

04Special visa

Visas with specific programme conditions, such as DTV (Destination Thailand Visa), LTR (Long-Term Resident), and PE (Privilege Entry), each with very different applicant eligibility requirements

05Next step

Once you know your group, the next step is to actually check your eligibility and documents. See details at Visa services or send your question to our team

This article is general information, not advice for a specific case. Criteria, documents, and processing times may differ depending on the type of application and announcements from the relevant authority. The outcome and approval timeframe are at the discretion of the government authority; PDC VISA cannot guarantee the outcome.
