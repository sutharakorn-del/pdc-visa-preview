Regulatory update

Summary of the approach under the Cabinet resolution of 14 Jul 2026, for foreign nationals in the resolution group of 11 Nov 2025.

Which group each worker is in, what must be submitted by when, and how far the permission extends — summarised as a timeline for employers

Information as of 14 Aug 2026may change according to official announcements

01Overview

According to the timeline diagram for implementing the Cabinet resolution dated 14 July 2026, foreign nationals under the Cabinet resolution of 11 November 2025 will receive an additional 1 year of permission from 11 December 2026 to 11 December 2027

Employers mustsubmit the work permit application for the foreign national by 11 December 2026 From 1 August 2026 onward, the work permit is valid through 11 December 2027

02Key timeline

  1. 11 Nov 2025Cabinet resolution that defines the group of foreign nationals

    The group referred to in this article

  2. 14 Jul 2026Cabinet resolution extending permission

    1-year extension, from 11 Dec 2026 to 11 Dec 2027

  3. 1 Aug 2026The date used to distinguish new-passport / old-passport cases

    and the starting date from which the work permit is valid through 11 Dec 2027

  4. 11 Dec 2026Deadline to submit the work permit application

    and the date used to distinguish stamp-transfer cases

  5. 30 Jun 2027Deadline for visa stamping / extension of stay

    The first stamp is valid through 30 Jun 2027, and the second-stamp extension must be requested by the same date

  6. 11 Dec 2027Permission ends

    The second stamp is valid through this date

03Which group is your worker in?

CaseStatusWhat to do
Group 1
New passport, issued after 1 Aug 2026		Not yet visa-stamped / visa not yet renewedGet the first stamp / extension of stay within 90 days of the passport's issue date, and no later than 30 Jun 2027 → then apply for the second-stamp extension through 11 Dec 2027
Group 1
Existing passport, issued before 1 Aug 2026		Not yet visa-stamped / visa not yet renewedGet the first stamp / extension of stay by 30 Jun 2027 → then apply for the second-stamp extension through 11 Dec 2027
Group 1
Existing passport, issued before 1 Aug 2026		Already visa-stamped / renewed to match the passport's validityStamp transfer: before 11 Dec 2026, permission matches the original entitlement · after 11 Dec 2026 or after the original visa ends, the stamp matches the passport's validity without extending the entitlement, then the first stamp must be completed by 30 Jun 2027
Group 2Already visa-stamped / renewed, with permission through 11 Dec 2026Apply for the second-stamp extension by 30 Jun 2027 → permission extends through 11 Dec 2027 · the stamp can be transferred to a new passport
