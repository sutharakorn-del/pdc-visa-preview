Regulatory update

60-day visa exemption ends: from 15 Sep 2026, 30-day stays for 60 countries

The 60-day visa exemption ended on 15 Sep 2026 — who now gets how many days, and what to do if you need to stay longer

Information as of 24 Sep 2026may change according to official announcements

01What changed

From 15 September 2026 the 60-day visa exemption ended and was replaced by a new framework under Ministry of Interior announcements published in the Royal Gazette on 31 August 2026.

SchemeWho it applies to
30-day visa exemptionPassport holders of 60 countries and territories, such as the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan, France and Germany
15-day visa exemptionSeychelles and Mauritius
Visa on ArrivalAzerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia

02Entering by land

Travellers using the 30-day exemption may generally enter Thailand through land border checkpoints under this exemption no more than twice per calendar year, except holders of Malaysian, Bruneian, Indonesian and Singaporean passports.

03If you need to stay longer

A visa exemption suits short tourist trips. For a longer stay — for family, retirement, study or work — plan a visa type that matches your purpose. See an overview under Visa services or talk to our team.

For the full country list and the latest conditions, please check with the Immigration Bureau or an embassy before you travel.

—Sources

  • TAT Newsroom — Thailand introduces new 30-day and 15-day visa exemption rules from 15 September — Tourism Authority of Thailand, 1 Sep 2026
