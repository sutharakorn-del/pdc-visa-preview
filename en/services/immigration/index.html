01What this service is

During their stay in Thailand, foreign nationals have periodic matters to handle with the Immigration Bureau. Some have deadlines, and some affect the status of stay if not carried out.

PDC VISA helps manage these matters on time and in full.

02Who it suits

  • Foreign nationals staying long-term in Thailand
  • Homeowners or accommodation providers who need to submit residence notifications for foreign nationals
  • Those who need to travel abroad while their permission to stay is still valid

03What PDC VISA can help with

  • 90-day report (form TM.47)
  • Residence notification for foreign nationals (Section 38 · form TM.30)
  • Residence certificate For obtaining a driving licence or buying a vehicle
  • Re-entry Permit Single and Multiple types
  • Extension of stay, together with Visa services

04General process

  1. Tell us what you needSpecify the type of request and the relevant deadline
  2. Preparing documentsOur team will inform you of the required documents for that request
  3. Filing the applicationCarried out with the Immigration Bureau.
  4. Deliver the resultDeliver documents and inform you of the next deadline (if any)

05Information usually needed

  • Passport and latest stay-permission stamp
  • Current residential address information
  • Travel details (for Re-entry)

Note — this list is general information. The documents actually required depend on the type of application and each person's status. Our staff will confirm what to prepare after an initial assessment.

06Points to note

Criteria, documents and processing times may vary depending on the type of application and the announcements of the relevant agency. The decision and approval time are within the authority of the government agency; PDC VISA cannot guarantee the outcome.

07Next step

Tell us the type of request and the relevant date. Our team will advise on the approach and fees.

08Frequently asked questions

If I'm travelling abroad, do I need a Re-entry Permit?

If you want to keep your existing permission to stay when you re-enter, you generally need to apply for a Re-entry Permit before travelling. Our team will help determine whether Single or Multiple suits your travel plans.

What is the residence certificate used for?

Used to support applications for a driving licence or purchasing a vehicle in Thailand
