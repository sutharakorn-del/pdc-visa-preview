01What this service is

A work permit is the document that allows a foreign national to work in Thailand under the specified conditions. Applying for, renewing, and amending it must also align with the worker's residence status or visa.

PDC VISA helps check your status, prepare documents, and coordinate so both matters move in the same direction.

02Who it suits

  • Employers who need to apply for or renew a work permit for an employee or foreign worker.
  • Foreigners working in Thailand who need help with documents.
  • Anyone changing employer, or whose work details have changed.

03When you might need it

  • Starting to employ a foreign national.
  • The work permit is nearing expiry.
  • Changing employer
  • An entry in the work permit needs to be amended or added.

04What PDC VISA can help with

  • Applying for a work permit
  • Renew a work permit (work permit renewal)
  • Work permit renewal together with passport and visa in one process, for workers covered by a Cabinet resolution
  • Changing employer — carried out with the Employment Office.
  • amending entries in the work permit — carried out with the Employment Office.
  • Advising on the visa and residence status related to the work.

05General process

  1. Assessing statusChecking the existing work permit, visa, and passport.
  2. Preparing documentsGathering documents from the employer and the worker.
  3. Filing the applicationFiling with the relevant agency according to the type of application.
  4. Following upTracking status and reporting the outcome.

06Information usually needed

  • Employer and establishment details.
  • The worker's passport and proof of residence.
  • Existing work permit (if any).
  • Details of the position or nature of the work.

Note — this list is general information. The documents actually required depend on the type of application and each person's status. Our staff will confirm what to prepare after an initial assessment.

07Points to note

Criteria, documents and processing times may vary depending on the type of application and the announcements of the relevant agency. The decision and approval time are within the authority of the government agency; PDC VISA cannot guarantee the outcome.

08Next step

Let us know the type of work you need (new application, renewal, employer change, or amendment) and the approximate number of people. Our staff will get back to you with an approach and a quote.

09Frequently asked questions

How far in advance can a work permit be renewed?

It depends on the type of permit and the announcement in effect at the time. Our staff will check the deadline for each case.

What needs to be done when changing employer?

You need to process the employer change with the Employment Office, and check whether your visa status still aligns with the new job.

What are the fees?

It depends on the type of application and the number of people. Please request a quote through the form.
