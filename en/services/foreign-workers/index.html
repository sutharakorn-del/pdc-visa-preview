01What this service is

Employing foreign workers lawfully involves several steps and several agencies, from registration, health checks, biometric registration, and applying for a work permit, through to the passport and visa stamping.

PDC VISA helps organise this work into clear steps, prepares documents, and coordinates on behalf of the employer within the agreed scope, for both MOU-group workers and workers under a Cabinet resolution group.

PDC VISA is operated by Ekarit Service Co., Ltd., holder of licence no. นจ.0427/2569 to operate a foreign-worker employment agency (bringing foreign workers to work for employers in Thailand), issued by the Department of Employment — View licence

02Who it suits

  • Factories, companies and SMEs that hire workers of Myanmar, Lao or Cambodian nationality.
  • HR departments that need to manage documentation for many workers at once.
  • Employers in domestic work and agriculture.
  • Employers with workers in a Cabinet resolution group who must act by the deadline.

03When you might need it

  • Taking on new workers who are not yet registered.
  • A worker's work permit is nearing expiry.
  • A worker needs a new passport or a visa stamp.
  • There is a new Cabinet resolution or announcement affecting your workers.
  • A worker changes employer, or an entry in the work permit needs amending.

04What PDC VISA can help with

  • Registering new workers — registration, health check and health insurance, biometric registration, and applying for a work permit.
  • Renewing work permits For workers who already have a passport and work permit.
  • Passport and visa for workers, including transferring the stamp to a new passport.
  • Changing employer andamending entries in the work permit
  • ID card for persons without registration status
  • Assessing the Cabinet resolution group to determine what each worker must do, and by which date.

05Types of MOU

  • New MOU workers — bringing in new workers from their home country through the MOU system
  • MOU Return / Recycle — for workers who have completed their MOU employment term and are returning to work for an employer in Thailand
  • MOU renewal for years 3–4 and 5–6 — renewing the work permission and visa of MOU workers at each stage
  • Recruitment — sourcing Myanmar, Lao and Cambodian workers through the MOU system to match the qualifications the employer needs

Conditions and timelines for each type of MOU depend on official announcements and the home country. Our team will check which type applies to your workers.

06General process

  1. RegistrationThe employer or an authorised representative submits the application through the electronic system.
  2. Health check and health insuranceIf covered by social security, register as an insured person; if not, take out health insurance.
  3. Biometric registrationCarried out with the Immigration Bureau.
  4. Submitting documents and awaiting approvalSubmitting additional documents, such as a medical certificate and proof of health insurance.
  5. Visa stampingCarried out according to the group and the announced deadline.

The duration of each step is set by the announcement for each round. See dated information in Knowledge & updates

07Information usually needed

  • Details of the employer or establishment, and the coordinator.
  • A list of workers, their nationality, and each person's current document status.
  • Workers' passports or identity documents (if any).
  • The existing work permit, for renewals or amendments.

Note — this list is general information. The documents actually required depend on the type of application and each person's status. Our staff will confirm what to prepare after an initial assessment.

08Points to note

Criteria, documents and processing times may vary depending on the type of application and the announcements of the relevant agency. The decision and approval time are within the authority of the government agency; PDC VISA cannot guarantee the outcome.

Criteria for foreign workers often change with Cabinet resolutions and agency announcements. Some deadlines directly affect a worker's rights, so each worker's status should be checked before the deadline.

10Frequently asked questions

Which worker nationalities does PDC VISA cover?

Documentation for workers of Myanmar, Lao and Cambodian nationality.

How long does it take?

It depends on the type of work, the worker's group, and the deadline set by each round's announcement. Our staff will give you an approximate timeline after checking your document status; the processing time itself is set by the government agency.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the worker's group, document status, and number of workers. Please request a quote through the form for organisations.

Our workers are in a Cabinet resolution group — what should we do?

See the dated summary of the approach in our regulation updates article and contact our staff for an individual assessment.
