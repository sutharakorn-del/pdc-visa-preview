01What this service is

Many visa and work permit applications depend on the accuracy of the passport and supporting documents. When a passport expires or is renewed, the status recorded in the old passport needs to be carried forward.

PDC VISA helps coordinate passport matters and prepares documents ready for your next application.

02Who it suits

  • Foreign nationals who need to apply for or renew a passport
  • Those who have a new passport and need to transfer their stamp
  • Employers who need to manage workers' passports and visas
  • Those who want help preparing and reviewing documents before submission

03What PDC VISA can help with

  • Coordinating passport applications or renewals (Passport Services)
  • New passports for workers Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia
  • Visa stamp transfer from the old passport to the new one
  • Embassy letter.
  • Preparing and reviewing supporting documents (Document Support)

04Passports for Myanmar, Lao and Cambodian workers

For employers of Myanmar, Lao or Cambodian workers, PDC VISA coordinates new passports for workers and follows through with the visa, both for new registrations and for work permit renewals.

  • Newly registered workers who need a passport
  • The current passport has expired or is about to expire
  • Workers under a Cabinet resolution who must obtain a new passport by a set deadline

05Got a new passport? What comes next

  1. Transfer a visa stamp to a new passportTransfer the permission-to-stay stamp from the old passport to the new one so the record of status continues.
  2. Check the work permit detailsCheck whether the work permit details need amending to match the new passport.
  3. Check the deadlinesFor workers under a Cabinet resolution, the passport issue date and the stamp-transfer date may affect how long permission lasts.

06General process

  1. Check document statusReview the validity of your passport and current stamp
  2. Preparing documentsPrepare documents according to the type of application
  3. Submit and coordinateCoordinate with the relevant authority or embassy
  4. Deliver and advise on next stepsInform you of the result and what to do next

07Information usually needed

  • Current and previous passport (for stamp transfers)
  • Documents related to your stay or work status

Note — this list is general information. The documents actually required depend on the type of application and each person's status. Our staff will confirm what to prepare after an initial assessment.

08Points to note

Criteria, documents and processing times may vary depending on the type of application and the announcements of the relevant agency. The decision and approval time are within the authority of the government agency; PDC VISA cannot guarantee the outcome.

For workers in a Cabinet resolution group, the passport issue date and the stamp transfer date may affect the length of permission. See the dated summary of guidelines

09Next step

Tell us which document service you need. Our team will let you know the required documents and approach.

10Frequently asked questions

Why transfer the stamp?

When you change to a new passport, the stay-permission stamp in the old passport must be transferred to the new one so the evidence of your status remains continuous.

Do I need to send a copy of my passport when making an initial inquiry?

No. At the initial inquiry stage, just tell us the type of service you need. Our team will ask for documents once the work actually begins.
