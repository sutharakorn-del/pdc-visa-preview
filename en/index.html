Every step has its details, every documentmatters

Visa, work permit and foreign-worker documentation involves several agencies, such as the Immigration Bureau, the Employment Office and the Department of Provincial Administration. Each application has its own conditions and documents.

PDC VISA's role is to give you the overview, let you know what to prepare, and handle coordination at each step — while the decision to approve always remains with the relevant authority.

How it works

Five steps,
from your first question
to following up on the result

Every case starts with understanding your situation, then arranging it into a single, clear path.

  1. 01

    Consultation and listening

    Tell us your situation, current status, and what you need. Our staff will help identify which service group your case falls under.

  2. 02

    Document review

    Reviewing the documents you have, identifying what is missing, and pointing out anything to correct before filing.

  3. 03

    Planning

    Summarising the steps, the agencies involved, and the order of action, together with a quote for your case.

  4. 04

    Filing and coordination

    Preparing the application and coordinating with the relevant agencies within the agreed scope of work.

  5. 05

    Following up

    Tracking the status of your application and keeping you informed of progress along the way.

Criteria, documents and processing times may vary depending on the type of application and the announcements of the relevant agency. The decision and approval time are within the authority of the government agency; PDC VISA cannot guarantee the outcome.

Scope of work covered

    New workers · Return / Recycle · renewal for years 3–4 and 5–6 · recruitment

    Registration, health check and health insurance, biometric registration, and applying for a work permit.

    Coordinating passport issuance, visa stamping, and transferring the stamp to a new passport.

  • Changing employer · Amending entries

    Helping assess which group each worker falls under, and what must be done by the deadline.

  • ID card for persons without registration status

    Coordinating the card application with the Department of Provincial Administration.

For individuals

Visas start with two questions:
how long will you stay in Thailand, and for what purpose?

Short-stay visa

For short-term travel and stays.

  • Tourism
  • Visiting family
  • Embassy letter

Long-stay visa

For continuous stays for reasons of family, work, education, or retirement.

  • Thai spouse
  • Supporting a Thai child
  • Dependant
  • Work
  • Retirement
  • Student

Special visa

A specific visa category with the conditions of its own programme.

  • DTV
  • LTR
  • PE

The visa type suited to you depends on your individual qualifications and documents. This information is an overview to help you get started, not a confirmation of eligibility.
